A team built around two things that shouldn't fail: security and power.

ADC Systems started as a small installation crew and grew into a full-service technical partner for surveillance, solar energy, hardware and web.

Our Story

From one camera install to a full systems company

ADC Systems was founded by engineers who kept hearing the same two complaints from clients: cameras that stopped recording when it mattered, and generators that couldn't keep up with the cost of fuel. We built our first business around fixing both, with properly configured surveillance and solar power that's sized to actually last.

As clients trusted us with more, they started asking us to source their equipment directly and build the websites that represented their business online. Today those four service arms, CCTV, solar, equipment and web, operate as one coordinated team, so you're never stuck coordinating between separate contractors.

We maintain, not just install
