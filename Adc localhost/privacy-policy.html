Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it secure.

We keep this site simple and secure. This policy describes web interactions only, including form submissions and analytics-related preferences.

1. Information we collect

When you visit ADC Systems, we may collect information that helps us deliver the site and respond to requests:

  • Exact entries submitted through contact or quote request forms, including name, email, phone number, service details and location.
  • Technical data from browser requests such as device type, browser version and IP address to keep the site secure and operational.
  • Cookie consent status and website preferences to remember your decision and improve site navigation.

2. Why we collect it

We use collected information only for clearly stated, legitimate business purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries, schedule site surveys, and provide accurate service quotes.
  • To maintain website performance, security and compatibility across devices.
  • To respect your cookie choices and ensure the site operates within the scope you consent to.

3. Cookies and local storage

ADC Systems uses essential browser storage to keep the website functioning normally and to remember your cookie preference.

  • Essential cookies/local storage: required for basic site behavior, secure page navigation, and keeping your consent decision.
  • Non-essential cookies: not set unless you explicitly accept them. If you reject non-essential cookies, the site continues to work with essential functionality only.

4. How we protect your data

We protect your information using modern website security practices and limit access to authorized team members only. Your personal data is not sold, rented, or shared with unrelated third parties.

5. Contact and updates

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle data, contact us directly at info@adcsystems.com. We may update this policy from time to time, and changes take effect when posted on this page.
